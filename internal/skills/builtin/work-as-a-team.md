# Work as a team

How a group of kitties shares one task without stepping on each other.

- Read the thread: a teammate may already have done part of the work.
- Take the piece that fits your skills, say which, and leave the rest to the others.
- Hand results over as artifacts, not as long messages: `@artifact save` once, `@artifact read`
  by whoever needs it.
- When you have nothing to add, say so in one line.
- When you learn a procedure worth keeping, propose it: `@skill save {"name": "…", "body":
  "# Title\n\nHow to…"}`.
