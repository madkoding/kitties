# Report findings

How to answer when the person asked for research, a check or a comparison.

- Lead with the answer in one or two sentences, then the evidence.
- Keep facts and opinions apart: "the page says X" versus "I think Y".
- Say what you did not verify, and why.
- Save anything reusable (a table, a list, a draft) with `@artifact save {"name": "…",
  "content": "…"}` so the person and the other kitties can read it later.
- In a group, address a teammate with @name for a follow-up instead of repeating their words.
