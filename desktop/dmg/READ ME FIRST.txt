KITTIES
=======

1. Drag Kitties.app onto the Applications folder.

2. macOS will say it "could not verify" Kitties. The app is not damaged:
   it is simply not signed with an Apple Developer ID yet. Open Terminal
   and run this once:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Kitties.app

3. Open Kitties from Applications. It carries its own gateway and starts it
   by itself: there is nothing else to install. In Settings add a model (an
   AI key) to give the kitties a brain; with Docker running they also get a
   desktop of their own, and without it they work brain-only.

   https://github.com/madkoding/kitties


KITTIES (en español)
====================

1. Arrastra Kitties.app a la carpeta Aplicaciones.

2. macOS dirá que no pudo verificar Kitties. La app no está dañada: aún no
   está firmada con un Developer ID de Apple. Abre Terminal y ejecuta esto
   una sola vez:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Kitties.app

3. Abre Kitties desde Aplicaciones. Trae su propio gateway y lo inicia solo:
   no hay que instalar nada más. En Ajustes agrega un modelo (una clave de
   IA) para darles cerebro a los kitties; con Docker encendido tienen además
   un escritorio propio, y sin él trabajan solo con su cerebro.
